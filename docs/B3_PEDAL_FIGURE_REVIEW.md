# B3 pedal primary-source review: figure boundaries

Source: Hammond Organ Service Manual, Early Models, https://medias.audiofanzine.com/files/hammond-service-manual-a-a-100-ba-bc-bcv-bv-b2-b3-c-cv-c2-c2-text-470145.pdf

## Confirmed from source text
- PDF page 45 (zero-based 44), printed page 2-20: later 25-pedal consoles use eight contacts; early models use seven. Four wires connect pedal busbars to pedal drawbars, optionally via a resistor panel. A choke and resistor filter the lower drawbar.
- PDF page 46 (zero-based 45): Figure 7 gives the physical arrangement for all other 25-pedal consoles: upper springs 10th, 6th, 2nd, fundamental; lower 12th, 8th, 4th, 3rd.
- The same manual explicitly says Figure 13 shows frequency numbers on each pedal contact and that Figures 8–11 depend on console serial number.
